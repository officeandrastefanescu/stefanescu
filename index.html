The 3 Core Shifts

When sessions go off track, most facilitators panic — and the room feels it immediately.

Staying grounded is about knowing where to put your attention. 

3 shifts that change everything:

  1. Notice where your attention goes the moment things get messy
  2. Shift your focus to what you can actually influence
  3. Respond to what the group needs — clearly, calmly, with presence

That's how you stay grounded in yourself — so the room can follow your lead
